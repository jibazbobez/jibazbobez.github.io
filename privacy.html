Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 8, 2026

At Andrew R. Tools (accessible from andrewr.online), your privacy is our priority. This document outlines the types of information we collect and how we use it.

1. Software & Plugins (Local Execution)

We build tools for professionals who value security. All products developed by Andrew R. Tools (including Arrange Master) operate with the following principles:

  • Local Only: All calculations and data processing happen entirely on your device.
  • No Telemetry: Our products do not track your usage, feature clicks, or artwork data.
  • Offline by Design: No internet connection is required to run our tools, and no data is transmitted to our servers from inside the software.

2. Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our products and user experience. GA4 collects data such as:

  • Anonymized IP addresses (generalized location).
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site.
  • Referral sources (how you found us).

This data is used solely for internal optimization and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our website. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept Cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our website. By dismissing the Cookie Banner on our site, you consent to the use of analytics cookies.

4. Third-Party Payments

Andrew R. Tools does not process or store your financial information. All transactions are handled securely by professional third-party marketplaces and payment gateways, including:

  • Lava.Top
  • Hipolink
  • Creative Market

Please review the privacy policies of these platforms for details on how they manage your billing data.

5. GDPR and Your Data Protection Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access, update or to delete the information we have on you.
  • The right of rectification. You have the right to have your information rectified if that information is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The right to object. You have the right to object to our processing of your Personal Data.

Since we do not collect personal accounts or emails (unless you directly email us for support), the only data associated with you is generalized analytics data. If you wish to opt-out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For any questions regarding this policy or our tools, please reach out directly:
