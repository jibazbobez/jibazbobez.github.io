Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 8, 2026

At Andrew R. Tools (accessible from andrewr.online), your privacy is our priority. This document outlines the types of information we collect and how we use it.

1. Software & Plugins (Local Execution)

We build tools for professionals who value security. All Adobe Illustrator plugins developed by Andrew R. Tools (including Arrange Master) operate with the following principles:

  • Local Only: All calculations and data processing happen entirely on your machine.
  • No Telemetry: Our plugins do not track your usage, feature clicks, or artwork data.
  • Offline by Design: No internet connection is required to run our tools, and no data is transmitted to our servers from inside the software.

2. Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our products and user experience. GA4 collects data such as:

  • Anonymized IP addresses (generalized location).
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site.
  • Referral sources (how you found us).

This data is used solely for internal optimization and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

We use "cookies" to enhance your experience. A cookie is a small file stored on your device that helps us recognize you on future visits. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some website features may not function as intended.

4. Third-Party Payments

Andrew R. Tools does not process or store your financial information. All transactions are handled securely by professional third-party marketplaces and payment gateways, including:

  • Lava.Top / Hipolink
  • Creative Market / Envato (GraphicRiver)

Please review the privacy policies of these platforms for details on how they manage your billing data.

5. GDPR & Your Rights

If you are a resident of the EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete the personal data we hold about you (mostly limited to support emails). For analytics, you can opt-out by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Contact Information

For any questions regarding this policy or our tools, please reach out directly:
