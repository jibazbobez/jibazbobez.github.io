03 / Layer Structure Retention

A tighter layout.
The same layer structure.

Print, cut, white ink, engraving, guides, QR codes: a dense layout should not turn your production document into a cleanup job.

Your layers stay your layers.

Arrange Master PRO recognises the layer structure of the selected artwork and retains it when placing the objects. Related production elements remain aligned rather than being flattened into a single layer.

The workflow is not limited to a fixed number of print and cut layers. Keep the document organised, include the related elements in the preparation, and check the result in the preview.

  • Print artwork and cut paths remain registered
  • Existing layer names and order stay meaningful
  • Guides, codes and production marks stay with the job

10 / Questions before production

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Arrange Master PRO?

Arrange Master PRO is a true-shape nesting plugin for Adobe Illustrator. It places selected artwork inside rectangular or custom containers, with spacing and rotation controls, while retaining the document’s layer structure. It is intended for print, sign, sticker and laser-cutting workflows.

How is PRO different from Arrange Master?

Arrange Master is a one-time purchase for individual designers and smaller workshops. PRO is a subscription for production teams that need more control over dense contour nesting in Illustrator. Both include Single Item Fill. Test your own jobs before choosing: a simpler layout may be enough for your work.

Does PRO replace my RIP or cutter software?

No. PRO prepares the arrangement in Illustrator. You still use your RIP, printer, laser or cutter software for colour management, output settings and machine control.

What happens to print, cut and guide layers?

PRO moves the related artwork while retaining its layer structure. Prepare related artwork at the same position, keep reference layers clearly organised, and inspect the final registration before output. Layer retention does not repair artwork that was already misaligned.

Can I try every feature before subscribing?

Yes. The demo provides the full plugin for 24 hours from first activation on one computer. Request it on the demo page; the key and download link arrive by email. No payment is required. A device or email that has already used a trial may not be eligible for another.

How do I buy and activate a subscription?

Choose Solo, Studio or Team on Lava.Top using an email address you can access. After the payment notification is processed, your key is sent to that email. Open Settings → License in the plugin and activate it. A paid key replaces an active demo on the device immediately.

Does cancelling renewal stop the current paid period?

Cancelling renewal does not remove the period already paid for. Check the start and expiry dates in your license details. Renewal is controlled by Lava.Top; the plugin checks the license’s current validity.

Is an internet connection required?

Yes, for activation, license checks and loading the current engine. Arrange Master PRO is not offered as an offline license.

Will the calculator match my production savings?

The calculator estimates material cost from your inputs. It is not a benchmark or a savings guarantee. Shape mix, margins, grain direction, machine limits and reusable offcuts affect the result. Measure the same job before and after during the demo.

Can I move a license to another computer?

A license has a device limit: Solo 1, Studio 3 or Team 8. Settings → License includes Release this device to remove that activation and free a slot. It does not change the computer’s hardware identity or reset trial eligibility. Contact support if you cannot access the old computer.
