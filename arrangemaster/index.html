02 / Four layout methods

Choose how the objects
fit the available space.

Use a grid for ordered collections, rectangular bounds for separation, or vector outlines for closer fitting. Each method has its own spacing and rotation controls.

Dense Packing

Fit pieces closer by working with their outlines. Useful for mixed sticker packs, cut parts and irregular illustrations that would leave gaps if treated as rectangles.

Start with
One rectangular container, your minimum spacing and allowed rotations.
Then adjust
Increase Genetic Algorithm effort when a tighter result is worth more time. Watch the best layout update in the preview.
Also included
Single Item Fill, Disperse and Align Final Edge.

Grid Layout

Put each object in a cell. Build icon sheets, catalog collections and repeating compositions with a regular visual rhythm.

Start with
The row and column count. A grid draft appears in the panel before you arrange.
Then adjust
Offset alternate rows, use square cells or introduce size and rotation variation.
Remember
A piece must fit its cell unless Size Limit Off is enabled.

Bounds Packing

Arrange using the rectangle around each object. Use it for images and design elements that need clear rectangular separation.

Start with
0° rotation for upright images, then choose a fill direction and spacing.
Then adjust
Use Fit to Container if all objects may be scaled together.
Remember
Transparent pixels in a raster image do not become a cut contour.

Shape Respect

Place artwork inside a letter, logo or another custom container. Follow its actual outline, including holes and separate regions.

Start with
A clean container boundary and enough source artwork to fill it.
Then adjust
Change spacing and allowed rotations; enable Disperse to spread objects more evenly across the shape.
Also included
Single Item Fill for repeating one design throughout the usable shape.

03 / Single Item Fill

Fill a sheet with copies
of one design.

Single Item Fill repeats a sticker, label or cut part inside an Adobe Illustrator container, using your spacing and allowed rotations.

Start with one design.

Select one grouped design and its container. Arrange Master creates and packs the copies for you.

Use a sheet or a custom shape.

Use Dense Packing for sticker sheets and label layouts, or Shape Respect to repeat artwork inside a letter, logo or another outline. Holes stay empty.

Check the copies before you commit.

Check the copy count and clearances in the preview. Apply keeps the result; Cancel restores your artwork.

04 / Disperse

Spread objects evenly
across a container.

Disperse balances the space between selected objects in Adobe Illustrator. Use it for scattered motifs, decorative collages and illustrations inside lettering.

Make space part of the composition.

Spread different shapes across the usable area to create an open arrangement for sticker packs, decorative graphics or a logo filled with motifs.

Follow the container’s outline.

Use Disperse in Dense Packing for rectangles, or Shape Respect for custom shapes with holes. It distributes your selection without creating copies.

Adjust spacing and compare the result.

Spacing varies with the shapes and available space. Set the minimum gap and permitted rotations, then inspect the preview and any items in Overflow.

05 / Artwork & preparation

Arrange mixed artwork
and prepared layers.

Version 1.6.0 accepts vector objects, raster images and mixed selections in the same workflow. Prepare each design as a complete object before arranging.

One selection for mixed artwork.

Paths, text, groups, symbols, clipping masks and images can be arranged together. Raster images use their rectangular bounds. Opacity masks are supported too.

Keep print and cut parts together.

For layered files, align the parts of each design directly above each other. Group the print artwork or use a clipping mask, and keep its cut line within the print bounds.

Inspect the file before output.

Check print and cut registration in the finished Illustrator artwork before printing or cutting. Save the document after applying the layout.

Review the layout
before you apply it.

The panel preview shows placement and optimization progress. You can change settings and arrange again within the session, then finish with Apply or Cancel.

01

Save your project and keep only one document open when possible. Do not open or switch documents during arrangement or an active session.

02

Select artwork and a container.

The largest selected object defines the available space. Group everything that should move as one design before starting.

03

04

05

11 / Frequently asked questions

Using Arrange Master.

Answers about artwork, sessions, activation and the differences between the ordinary and PRO editions.

How do Arrange Master and Arrange Master PRO differ?

Arrange Master includes four layout methods, Single Item Fill, Disperse and a panel preview with a one-time lifetime license. Arrange Master PRO adds automatic additional containers and a large preview for multi-sheet work, with subscription plans.

Which method should I use for a sticker sheet?

Use Dense Packing to fit different irregular outlines into a rectangle. Use Single Item Fill for copies of one design. Choose Grid Layout when equal cells and an ordered presentation matter more than close fitting.

Will Arrange Master resize my artwork?

Dense Packing and Shape Respect keep item dimensions. In Bounds Packing, Fit to Container scales the collection when enabled. Grid’s Vary Size also changes dimensions. Leave those sizing options off for dimension-critical work.

Can I mix images, text and vector objects?

Yes. The 1.6.0 workflow accepts mixed artwork, including clipping and opacity masks. Raster images use rectangular bounds. Keep all parts of a design grouped or prepared as aligned components before arranging.

Can I keep my cut lines on a separate layer?

Yes, with a prepared layered file. Keep related parts directly above each other, group the print artwork or place it under a clipping mask, and keep the cut line within its print bounds. Inspect registration before output.

When do the 24 hours start?

The trial starts at the first successful activation on one computer. Requesting the key or downloading the plugin does not start the clock. It includes all ordinary Arrange Master features. Reinstalling does not restart it.

What happens when the pieces do not all fit?

Unplaced items are collected above the container. Apply the layout, then use the remaining pieces with another container in a new session. Automatic additional containers are available in Arrange Master PRO.

What does the lifetime purchase include?

A paid license has no recurring subscription and includes free v1.x updates. Personal covers one computer, Team four and Extended Commercial ten. Choose the usage rights for your projects, then keep using the purchased version.

Does activation need an internet connection?

Yes. Activation, license checks and downloading or reloading the engine need a connection. Keep internet access available during the trial. Review the installation and connection guide if the panel cannot connect.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Finish the session, open Settings → License and select Release this device. Then activate on the other computer with the same key. Contact info@andrewr.online if the old computer is unavailable. Releasing a device does not renew a trial.
